2 entries
Hypothesis

Investor-Flow Absorption — who takes the other side of the foreign bid

Taiwan publishes something almost no other market does: a daily, per-security breakdown of who traded, split by investor category. Foreign buying is the most-watched number in the market and therefore worthless as an edge — but the counterparty is not watched at all. The claim: a large slice of foreign flow is mandated rather than informed, so the gap between local discretionary accumulation and the foreign bid predicts what neither flow predicts alone. With the scale trap that silently turns the residual back into a crowded idea, and the split-dealer-book test that could kill the framing outright.

/investor-flow-absorption -> Paper

R&D Investments & Past Returns
